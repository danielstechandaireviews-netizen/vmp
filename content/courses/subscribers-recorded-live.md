Most subscriber advice is someone describing what they think worked. This is a screen recording of it happening.

You've heard it all already — post consistently, make good thumbnails, be yourself. None of it tells you what to actually do on Monday morning, because nobody shows you the screen. This course is the opposite. Everything is recorded live: real channels, real analytics, real numbers moving — including the videos that failed and why.

- The analytics screen that tells you where subscribers actually come from

- What a channel's first 1,000 subscribers really look like week by week
- The things that feel productive and bring in zero subscribers

**What you'll be able to do:** read your own analytics with confidence, know which of your videos deserve more of your effort, fix the one thing that's costing you subscribers, and stop guessing what's working.
