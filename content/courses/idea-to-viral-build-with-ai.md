Your idea isn't going viral because nobody can use it yet.

Everyone has the idea. The person who wins is the one who put a working version in front of people first — a link someone can click, an app someone can open, a tool someone can try and then send to a friend. That used to take months and a developer's budget. It doesn't anymore.

This course shows you how to take an idea in your head and turn it into a real, live product using AI — a website, a web app, a mobile app, or full software — even if you have never written a line of code.

### What you'll be able to do by the end

- Take any idea from a sentence to something live on the internet
- Build websites, web apps, mobile apps and software using AI as your developer
- Understand the full build process end to end — plan, build, test, launch, improve
- Know the stack, the tools and what each one is actually for, without the jargon
- Ship fast enough to test several ideas instead of betting a year on one

**What's inside:** a step-by-step path from your first build to a launched product — the development lifecycle explained plainly, the tools and stacks that matter, and guided builds for web, mobile and software, all done with AI doing the heavy lifting.

**Who it's for:** anyone with an idea and a full-time life. No coding background needed. No computer science degree. Just a laptop and a few focused hours a week.

**Why this connects to going viral:** speed is the whole advantage. When you can build in days instead of months, you can test ten ideas in the time most people spend planning one — and you only need one of them to catch.

Stop describing your idea. Start shipping it.
